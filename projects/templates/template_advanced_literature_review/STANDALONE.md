# Standalone Fork Guide

The intended 0.2.0 package pins `research-project-template` in `pyproject.toml`
for its shared validator and platform adapters, and
`template-literature-meta-analysis==0.2.0` for its canonical literature,
bibliometric, knowledge-graph and reproducibility engines. The checkout's
`tool.uv.sources` entries resolve these dependencies to the public monorepo.
For an independent source clone, the explicit wheel recipe below installs the
matching SDK and Meta provider without consulting or editing that checkout-only
resolver. These are unreleased candidate packages; do not assume they exist on
PyPI. Installed-wheel acceptance separately exercises the actual distributions
outside the checkout; local preparation alone is not that acceptance.

The engine installs only `template_literature_meta_analysis.*`. Advanced owns
its configuration loader, phase boundaries, calibration, cross-phase provenance
and astronomy fixtures. Its public literature and behavior-verified generic
variable boundaries delegate to the declared engine. Additive license metadata
is retained, and Europe PMC repository records are not classified as preprints
merely because of their repository. The 90% gate measures all Advanced source;
the engine has its own independent all-source gate.

## Purpose

`template_advanced_literature_review` is the advanced phase-aware review exemplar:
search design, deterministic/optional model filtering, cross-phase validation
and manuscript generation. Its bundled exoplanet corpus is a generated synthetic
fixture, not a retrieved empirical evidence snapshot or a completeness claim.

## Copy This When

Use it whenever the research object is *a body of literature that has evolved through distinct methodological phases*: configure temporal boundaries and filtering criteria for each phase to get a reproducible, evidence-traceable meta-analysis with cross-phase validation and phase-aware manuscript statistics.

## Required Post-Fork Edits

### Core Configuration

- Set the domain and phases in `manuscript/config.yaml` → `project_config.search_phases` with appropriate temporal boundaries and methodological focus for your field.
- Update the `project_config.search.term`, `query` blocks, `relevance_keywords`, `subfield_keywords`, and `hypothesis_definitions` for the new domain.
- Configure each phase's deterministic filters and optional `llm_filters` in `project_config`.

### Domain Adaptation

- Create domain-specific subfield defaults: copy `data/subfield_defaults_exoplanet.yaml` to `data/subfield_defaults_<your_domain>.yaml` and update with your field's terminology.
- Update `CITATION.cff`, `.zenodo.json`, `codemeta.json`, and `pyproject.toml` (title, keywords, repository).
- Adapt the domain profile: copy and customize `domain_profile.yaml` for your field.

### Multi-Phase Corpus

First complete the [standalone environment](#standalone-environment), or prepare
the canonical own-project runtime through the

The generator prints its record count and destination; replay prints

The generator defaults to a different `data/fixtures/...` path unless `--out`

machinery, not real retrieval or scientific temporal findings. Do not regenerate

For a separately authorized live application, configure the review protocol and

replays a fixture. Retain actual engine outcomes and source provenance. Choose

## Multi-Phase Architecture

The advanced template extends the single-term approach with:

### Phase-Based Search Strategy
- **Foundation Phase**: Broad domain retrieval under configured year bounds
- **Focused Phase**: Technology- or method-specific retrieval
- **Synthesis Phase**: Phenomenon-specific retrieval and cross-phase validation
- Each phase has distinct temporal boundaries, search terms, and filtering criteria

### Advanced Filtering Pipeline
- **Deterministic filters**: Year boundaries, venue quality, citation thresholds
- **LLM-based filters**: Abstract content analysis for methodological relevance
- **Cross-phase validation**: Later phases validate/extend findings from earlier phases

### Unified Knowledge Synthesis
- All phases contribute to single RDF knowledge graph
- Phase provenance maintained throughout pipeline
- Cross-temporal hypothesis validation with statistical confidence intervals

## Pipeline Stages (11 Total)

1. **Multi-Phase Search** (`01_multi_phase_search.py`): Execute phase-based retrieval with temporal boundaries
2. **Meta-Analysis Pipeline** (`02_meta_analysis_pipeline.py`): Run deterministic corpus analysis
3. **Build Knowledge Graph** (`03_build_knowledge_graph.py`): Extract assertions with phase provenance
4. **Generate Figures** (`04_generate_figures.py`): Create phase-aware visualizations
5. **Inject Variables** (`05_inject_variables.py`): Compute phase statistics and cross-temporal metrics
6. **Fulltext Assessment** (`06_fulltext_assessment.py`): Assess PDF availability across phases
7. **Literature Evaluation** (`07_literature_evaluation.py`): Quality scoring with phase weighting
8. **Deep Research Dispatch** (`08_deep_research_dispatch.py`): deterministic provider-neutral fixture replay
9. **Export Bibliography** (`09_export_bibliography.py`): Generate phase-annotated bibliography
10. **Reproducibility Assessment** (`10_reproducibility_assessment.py`): Cross-phase workflow validation
11. **Full-Text Download** (`11_fulltext_download.py`): Explicit network enrichment before stage 10

## Fixture provenance and live evidence

The committed default corpus is synthetic. It demonstrates the artifact shape
and deterministic analysis path, not exoplanet findings, retrieval completeness
or freshness. A genuine live review must replace it deliberately, preserve
retrieval/phase provenance and keep the exact consumed corpus/configuration.

## What You Can Claim

- **Method patterns:** phase membership, filtering, overlap and configured integration checks demonstrated on retained inputs.
- **Fixture analytics:** descriptive calculations over generated records, not empirical history of a research field.
- **Evidence lineage:** actual recorded artifacts and Source bindings, with missing/fixture/live states kept distinct.
- **Domain claims:** only after independent source-backed review of a genuine live corpus; demonstration output alone is insufficient.

## What Not To Claim

- Do not present the bundled exoplanet corpus findings as novel domain discoveries without independent validation
- Do not claim completeness of coverage without systematic gap analysis
- Do not generalize cross-phase patterns to other fields without domain-specific validation

Advanced configuration loading is project-local. Its wheel includes config,
config_loader and config_validation alongside the multi-phase packages. The
previous sibling meta-analysis bootstrap alias is unnecessary; installing both
flat exemplar wheels into one environment is not the standalone acceptance
contract. Config-loader value/error/output parity was checked against the
previous actual loader before migration. Domain-specific configuration remains
in this exemplar, while only the identical validation functions use the declared
platform SDK dependency pinned in `pyproject.toml`.

## Abstract

Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) is an influential optimization technique widely used in the fields of machine learning and deep learning. This paper introduces the concept of Gradient Descent (GD), SGD, and Mini-Batch SGD with a specific focus on the comparison between these three similar algorithms via code implementations in large-sample regression analysis. The results show that Gradient Descent ensures comprehensive, albeit slow, progress. Stochastic Gradient Descent leads to the quickest convergence rate, albeit with high variability. Mini-batch Gradient Descent achieves a trade-off between computational efficiency and algorithmic stability. Our study provides a comprehensive analysis of many essential optimization approaches, highlighting their practical use and associated trade-offs. This research offers valuable theoretical comprehension for beginners.

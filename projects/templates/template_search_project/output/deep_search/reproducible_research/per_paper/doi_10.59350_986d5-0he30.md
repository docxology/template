## Abstract

When the Greek philosopher Heraclitus pronounced his famous &amp;#34;πάντα ῥεῖ&amp;#34; (everything flows), he most probably was not thinking about software. But it applies to software as much as to other aspects of life: software is in perpetual change, being modified to remove bugs, add features, and adapt it to changing environments.

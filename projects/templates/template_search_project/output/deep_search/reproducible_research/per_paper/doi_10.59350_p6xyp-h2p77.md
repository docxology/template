## Abstract

&lt;strong&gt; Researchers release a treasure trove of data on the developing retina, pushing the boundaries of neuroscience publishing by presenting it dynamically and reproducibly. &lt;/strong&gt; A new paper in &lt;em&gt; GigaScience &lt;/em&gt; today demonstrates a major step forward for reproducible research and public data-sharing in the neurosciences with the publication and release of a huge cache of electrophysiology data resources.

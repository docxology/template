# Native PDF mathematical observations

`native_format_pdf_math.anchored_equations_from_ast` compiles exact mathematical source strings and adjacent prose anchors from actual Pandoc JSON. `inspect_anchored_pdf_math` independently checks those obligations against original PDF glyphs and every admitted 150 dpi page image. Both APIs retain explicit unsupported obligations. They do not replace the complete manuscript source contract.

The finite grammar currently supports inline letters, digits, arithmetic and comparison signs, selected Greek and relation macros, one-atom superscripts/subscripts, and one-atom numerator/denominator `frac` or `tfrac`. Each mathematical source byte must belong to the grammar. Unknown commands, nested or repeated scripts, multi-atom fraction terms, display equations, and occurrences without independent adjacent prose anchors remain blocked.

The observer checks exact mathematical atoms in their original occurrence order, positive finite native glyph geometry, script size and vertical position, fraction numerator/denominator overlap and vertical position, actual fraction-bar pixels, equation crop bounds, and localized ink for every mathematical glyph. Unicode mathematical alphanumeric glyphs are normalized explicitly to their logical character; original native identities and positions remain in observations. Other Unicode characters are not compatibility-folded.

The actual native regression compiles ordinary XeLaTeX PDFs and renders every page with Poppler. It includes superscript and fraction baselines, changed mathematical atoms, a flattened superscript, a flattened fraction, a fraction with a genuinely absent rule, and invisible equations whose source glyphs remain extractable. Setup errors and unsupported obligations cannot satisfy a negative control. The regression is `tests/infra_tests/reporting/test_native_pdf_math.py`.

A source-only inspection of the current 25 public scopes found 1,256 mathematical nodes, 201 tables and 1,368 citation nodes. The initial finite grammar identified 496 independently anchored mathematical occurrences syntactically; that number is discovery and is not evidence that their native artifacts pass. A real hydrated SIA combined PDF independently passed both source equations, `n` and `n+1`, on page 5. Complete scientific manuscript acceptance still requires independently reconstructed raw markup, bibliography/citation rendering, table geometry, figures, display/complex mathematical structures, and manuscript slide content. Those blockers must remain until their own genuine native baselines and deliberate failure controls pass.

For portable admission, reconstruct the actual source AST from retained source bytes and source-owned renderer transformations, compile these obligations again, and recompute the observation from retained original PDF and page bytes. Compare the complete observation and original source identities. A producer-supplied equation list, region coordinates, success status, or a repeated producer parser result alone is insufficient.

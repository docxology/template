# Liberation asset instructions

Follow the [font instructions](../AGENTS.md). Preserve the unmodified font bodies
and original copyright and license notices in [OFL.txt](OFL.txt). Update
[provenance.json](provenance.json) from actual selected bodies when a reviewed
font change is made; do not hand-edit hashes to authorize a replacement.

See [README.md](README.md) for provenance and consumption requirements.
